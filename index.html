Short GTA 6 news videos, made and published in one place

gta6bot is the publishing tool of Six Actu, an independent French fan channel about GTA 6. It turns sourced news into vertical videos and posts them to the channel's YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

Features

Sourced news

Every fact in a video comes from Rockstar Games or a recognised news outlet, and the source is kept with the video.

Vertical video editing

Voice-over, word-by-word subtitles and clips laid out in 1080×1920 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

Quality check

Each video is scored before release; videos below the threshold are not published.

Multi-platform publishing

One video, posted to YouTube, Instagram and TikTok with a caption written for each platform.

AI disclosure

The voice-over is synthetic, so every TikTok post carries TikTok's "AI-generated content" label.

Owner approval

Nothing is posted without the channel owner reviewing the video and choosing to publish it.

How it works

  1. Pick a subject. gta6bot gathers the day's GTA 6 news from official and press sources.
  2. Write and edit. It drafts a short script, records the voice-over and assembles the vertical video with subtitles.
  3. Review. The video is scored, then the channel owner watches the preview and approves it.
  4. Publish. The approved video is uploaded to the connected accounts, each with its own caption.

Publishing to TikTok

gta6bot uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API with the scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok and authorises gta6bot. TikTok sends them back to the connection page.
  2. The owner watches the finished video and reads its caption before approving it.
  3. Before each post, gta6bot reads the creator's info from TikTok and uses the privacy level the owner has chosen, only if TikTok lists it among the options allowed for the account.
  4. The post is marked as AI-generated content, with comments, Duet and Stitch allowed, then sent to TikTok.

gta6bot only posts to the account that authorised it, never reads other users' data, and stores the access token on the owner's computer only. Access can be removed at any time from TikTok's app settings. Details are in the privacy policy.

YouTube and Instagram

Uses YouTube API Services. gta6bot uploads videos to the Six Actu channel with the youtube.upload scope. By using gta6bot you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Instagram Reels are published to the Six Actu professional account through Meta's Instagram API.

About

Six Actu is an independent fan channel, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. gta6bot is built and run by the channel for its own accounts.
